WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

How to read the run

Train

Fit parameters using 70% of the observed choices.

Validation

Compare ranks on a separate 15%. Lowest NLL selects the rank.

Test

After selection, evaluate once on the final 15%.

NLL = average −log(probability given to the chosen item). Lower is better. Each rank has new random initial factors for each restart; the data stay fixed. Fits use Adam with a small L2 penalty and early stopping. The scaling study instead fits full CDM at four nested sample sizes, repeats three trials, and plots mean centered matrix MSE with a 1/m guide.

About this guide. The Chai–Coffee–Juice worked example and its explanation are adapted from the original study notes of Nitesh Kumar Shah, developed under the supervision of Prof. Suryanarayana Sankagiri at IIT Madras. The CDM and low-rank model come from Seshadri, Peysakhovich, and Ugander (ICML 2019). These toy observations and initial factors are teaching examples, not data or results from the paper.
01 / CDM

Directed interactions

The paper writes uxz for context item z's contribution to target item x. Add contributions from the other items in the offered set:

utility(x | S) = ∑z∈S\{x} uxz

Then softmax turns all item utilities into probabilities. Editing the matrix in the first view changes this calculation immediately.

02 / LOW RANK

Learn two vectors per item

The low-rank construction replaces a separate number for every directed pair with a target vector and a context vector:

uxz = tx · cz

Rank r means r coordinates in each vector. The matrix view fits those vectors to an editable matrix; the experiment view learns them from simulated choices.

03 / IIA

What MNL cannot change

In MNL, adding C to {A, B} cannot change the odds P(A)/P(B). CDM can change those odds because C may affect A and B differently.

IIA: P(A | {A,B}) / P(B | {A,B}) = P(A | {A,B,C}) / P(B | {A,B,C})

The matrix studio shows these odds from the current interactions.

04 / HELD-OUT FIT

Why compare on new choices?

The paper compares out-of-sample negative log-likelihood for MNL and CDMs of different ranks on real data. Here you can recreate the question with new, seeded synthetic choices.

NLL = −mean log P(chosen item | offered set)

A lower training NLL alone does not establish that a higher rank predicts better. Inspect validation, then the final test comparison.

SCOPE OF THIS APP

Inspired by the paper, not a numerical reproduction

The experiment lab generates fresh synthetic data with adjustable settings. Its curves are not the paper's Figure 1–3, and it does not load SFWork, SFShop, nature photos, or the paper's mixed MNL comparison. Numerical results will differ with data, seed, sample size, optimization, and regularization.

For a faithful reproduction, use the original datasets and exact preprocessing, splits, baselines, optimization settings, and repeated trials. The app is a place to understand the mechanics before doing that work in a research notebook.
