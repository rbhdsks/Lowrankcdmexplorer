Selected rank RMSE —

RMSE is the typical size of a reconstruction mistake across the 12 off-diagonal cells. Smaller is closer to your matrix. This is matrix approximation, not a fit to observed choices or validation data.

Rank 3 can reach zero here because the four unused diagonal entries are free. Rank 4 can also be exact. More rank is not automatically better for prediction on new choice data.
